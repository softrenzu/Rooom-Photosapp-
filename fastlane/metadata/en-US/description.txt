RooomShot is a focused camera app that sends every photo you take straight to your own Google Drive.

There is no share sheet and no destination to select after every shot. Connect your Google Account, create a destination folder once, and use the large camera button whenever you need to capture something.

Key features
• Automatic Google Drive upload immediately after capture
• Safe on-device queue while offline
• Automatic retry when connectivity returns, plus manual retry
• Maximum, high-quality, and space-saving image options
• Clear queued, uploading, completed, and failed history
• Japanese and English interface

Privacy by design
Photos travel directly from your iPhone to your Google Drive. They never pass through a ROOOMTECH server. RooomShot requests only the Google Drive permission needed to manage files and folders it creates. It does not request access to read your entire Drive and includes no advertising trackers.

RooomShot is ideal for property photos, field work, inspections, construction records, inventory checks, and any workflow where capture and cloud storage should be one action.

Monthly plan
Photo capture and automatic Google Drive upload are available through a ¥500 monthly auto-renewable subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. The plan renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings.
